Fitting & sizing

No custom grinding. Just your working distance.

Traditional loupes are custom-built for one user. arLoupe comes in three sizes keyed to your working distance — eye to surgical site. Most users fit out of the box.

Working distance is the primary fitting variable; height ranges are a guide and overlap between sizes. Your specialist confirms the best fit during your demonstration.

Eye safety

Designed to be worn all day.

arLoupe's Micro OLED screens are self-luminescent — they don't rely on the external blue-LED backlight used in typical AR or smart glasses. As a result, blue-light output is very low and the risk of blue-light strain is considered negligible.
