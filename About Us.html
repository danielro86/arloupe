About Bewelltech

Medicine digitized everything — except the surgeon's own eyes.

We built arLoupe to close it — a wearable that turns the surgeon's magnified view into a live digital stream. Captured. Broadcast. Built to learn.

Our mission

To capture medicine from the provider's exact perspective — and unlock the insight to transform how surgery is performed, taught, and learned.

The last analog island

The medical black box.

Imaging, charting, scheduling — every part of the clinic went digital. Except the execution of medicine itself, still unrecorded from the one view that matters most: the surgeon's eyes.

Loupes and microscopes are analog islands. Surgeons can't review their work. Schools can't teach from the chair. The most valuable view in the room stays trapped behind glass.

"Optical glass is a dead-end for data."
What we built

arLoupe completely digitizes the surgical field.

A high-speed Sony sensor and dual Micro OLED screens replace ground glass — microscope-grade vision you can zoom, record, and broadcast live. It comes down to three things.
